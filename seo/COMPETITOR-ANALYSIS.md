# Competitor Analysis — TheTicketers

## Direct competitors (Dubai/UAE market)

### 1. Platinumlist.net — the "BookMyShow of the UAE"
- **Strength:** Official ticketing partner for most UAE events; massive brand searches; event schema everywhere; Arabic + English.
- **Weakness:** Weak editorial content; attraction coverage thinner than events.
- **Our angle:** Out-write them on guides ("what to wear desert safari", "coca-cola arena seating guide") and attraction comparisons they don't do.

### 2. Headout.com
- **Strength:** Aggressive programmatic SEO (thousands of "{attraction} tickets" pages), strong CWV, multilingual, heavy FAQ schema. Ranks top-3 for most "dubai tickets" queries.
- **Weakness:** Template fatigue — pages feel identical; little genuinely local detail.
- **Our angle:** Their template is our blueprint. Match the structure, beat them on local specificity (timings, metro, Ramadan notes).

### 3. GetYourGuide / Viator / Tiqets / Klook
- **Strength:** Domain authority 80+, reviews at scale, brand bidding.
- **Weakness:** Generic global templates; thin "things to do" editorial vs. their booking pages.
- **Our angle:** Don't fight head terms ("dubai tours"). Win the long tail and comparison queries ("getyourguide vs headout burj khalifa" style content is allowed and converts).

### 4. VisitDubai.com / Dubai Calendar
- **Strength:** Official authority; wins "dubai events" head terms.
- **Weakness:** No ticketing intent capture; no prices on page.
- **Our angle:** Price-led titles ("Dubai Events This Weekend — Tickets from AED 50").

### 5. TimeOut Dubai / What's On Dubai
- **Strength:** Editorial muscle, news freshness, social audience.
- **Weakness:** Affiliate links are an afterthought; listicles lack live pricing.
- **Our angle:** Their listicle topics + our live prices = better page for the same query.

## Keyword gaps to exploit (low-to-mid difficulty)
- "X tickets price 2026" patterns (price-intent, weak SERPs)
- "this weekend in dubai" / "dubai events today" (freshness wins, big players don't update)
- Seasonal: "dubai summer indoor activities", "ramadan events dubai", "NYE fireworks dubai tickets"
- Comparison: "burj khalifa 124 vs 148", "desert safari morning vs evening", "yas island vs dubai parks"
- Logistics: "how to get to global village", "coca-cola arena parking"

## US/UK cities — reality check
"broadway tickets", "vegas shows" SERPs are owned by Ticketmaster/StubHub/SeatGeek (DA 90+). Do NOT spend content budget there in months 1–6. Let programmatic event pages catch stray long-tail only.
